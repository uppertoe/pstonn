p.stonn is an unofficial app for scheduling a City of Stonnington visitor permit. It is not affiliated with the council. Your council password is stored encrypted. Everything else you enter, including registrations and your schedule, is stored unencrypted and can be read by the server administrator. There is more detail on the Security & data page.

- p.stonn stays signed in to your council account so it can change the permit while you are not there. You can disconnect it at any time in Settings.
- You are responsible for checking that the permit shows the correct registration. Notifications are not guaranteed, and p.stonn is not responsible for parking fines.
