Please read and tick each point to use p.stonn.

- I understand this is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Stonnington.
- I understand p.stonn keeps a live connection to my council account that it renews by itself, so it can change my permit while I am not using the app. It stops after 90 days unless I confirm it, and I can disconnect it at any time in Settings.
- I understand the app does its best to keep my council login safe, but that my council password is saved (encrypted) unless I turn that off, and that the person running this server could technically read what the server can read. I should not reuse this password for anything that matters.
- I understand that apart from my council credentials my data is stored unencrypted on the server — my plates, my schedule, my permit details and email addresses — and that copies persist for a while in backups after I delete my account.
- I understand that if I create a guest link or a printed door QR, other people can change my permit themselves, at a time of their choosing, without asking me each time.
- I understand that if I give someone shared access they can change my schedule, cars and guest passes, and that I can see who changed what on the Activity page.
- I understand the app does its best to notify me of my permit's status but cannot guarantee delivery, and that I remain responsible for checking my permit and for any fines.
