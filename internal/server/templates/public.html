About p.stonn

p.stonn is a free, unofficial tool that schedules changes to your City of Stonnington visitor parking permit. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the City of Stonnington. For a walk-through of what it does, see How it works.

The security model

  • p.stonn holds a live, encrypted session to your council account. That session can view and change your permit. It is encrypted at rest with a key held on this server.
  • This is necessary to reliably connect to your Stonnington ePermit account.
  • If this server, its database, or its encryption key were compromised, your council session could be exposed and misused. Encryption reduces this risk but does not remove it. No online service can promise perfect security.
  • You may choose to not store your council password — instead just your email, your schedule, and the encrypted session. Without your password, this app may get disconnected from your Stonnington ePermit account and lose the ability to modify your permit. If you opt in to auto-reconnect, your password is also kept, sealed with the same encryption, so the app can sign back in on its own; you can remove it any time in Settings.
  • Your p.stonn login email is verified by a one-time code, and you can only link the council account whose email matches it.
  • Please ensure that your Stonnington ePermit password is unique to your council account, so nothing else of yours is exposed if this service is ever compromised.
  • You can revoke p.stonn's access at any time: disconnect in Settings, or change your council password (which ends the stored session, usually within about half an hour).
  • Before using this service, you should consider whether you are comfortable with a third party holding access to your council account. Do not rely on it for anything you can't afford to have go wrong.

Your data

  • p.stonn stores what it needs to run and nothing more: your email, your vehicles and their plates, your schedule, your permit details, an activity log of changes it made, and the encrypted council session (plus your password, only if you opted in).
  • Your data is used only to operate this service for you. It is never sold, never shared with anyone, and never used for advertising. There are no trackers or analytics — the app makes no requests to any third party from your browser.
  • Honesty requires saying: the person running this server could technically read what the server can read. What you get is their commitment not to, this page saying so publicly, and the small, personal scale of the service.
  • “Delete all my data” in Settings does what it says, immediately and permanently: your session, schedule, vehicles, guest passes, and history are all removed.

Notifications

  • p.stonn attempts to notify you (by email and/or push) each time it changes your permit or fails to. This is best-effort: messages can be delayed, filtered, or lost, and the service can be down. Delivery is not guaranteed.
  • You remain responsible for checking your permit and for any fines. Treat notifications as a convenience rather than a guarantee.

For the City of Stonnington

If you work for the council and have questions or concerns about this service, please get in touch{{if .Contact}} via the contact form{{end}} — happy to talk. p.stonn acts only with each resident's own login, at their request, on their own permit, and is rate-limited to stay well below what a person clicking through ePermits would generate. If the council ever offers permit scheduling natively, or an official way for residents to delegate access, this app will adopt it or retire.

No promises

p.stonn is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. It may stop working, make mistakes, or lose data. Use it at your own risk.

Why this exists

Stonnington Council changed how resident and visitor parking permits work in 2026. Paper visitor permits have been phased out in favour of a digital system. The digital permit requires you to enter vehicle registration numbers into the council's online system, which is checked by parking officers as they patrol the streets.

The old paper permits could be moved between cars as needed. The digital permit holds one vehicle registration at a time, so a household that relies on regular help — grandparents doing childcare pickup, a nanny, a carer — has to log in and change the registration whenever the car changes. That quickly becomes a recurring chore.

p.stonn is an attempt to make that easier. Once you have a permit set up with Stonnington Parking, you can use it to set which car is on the permit each day of the week, add one-off bookings, and hand a visitor a QR link that puts their car on when they arrive.

I built it to take a chore off my household's plate. I hope it does the same for yours.

Before you start: get a Stonnington ePermit

p.stonn does not replace the council's system. It works on top of a permit you already hold, so you need a City of Stonnington ePermits account with at least one visitor permit first.

If you are not set up yet:

  • Check you are eligible. Permits are for residents in permit areas, and eligibility depends on your property (generally limited or no off-street parking).
  • Apply through the council. You can apply online, by email, by post, or in person at a customer service centre. You will usually need proof you live at the address and your vehicle details.
  • Set up your online account. The council's digital system is where permits are managed and vehicle registrations are entered.
  • Add a visitor permit. Once your account exists, apply for a visitor permit if you do not already have one. Fees apply, with a discount for concession cardholders.

The council's own pages have the current fees, forms and details:

  • Parking permits
  • Visitor parking permits
  • Digital parking permit FAQs

Once your council account has a visitor permit, sign in to p.stonn and connect it.
