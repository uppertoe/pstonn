About p.stonn

p.stonn is a free, unofficial tool that schedules which vehicle is on your City of Stonnington visitor parking permit. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the City of Stonnington. For a walk-through of what it does, see How it works.

The security model, plainly

  • p.stonn holds a live, encrypted session to your council account. That session can view and change your permit. It is encrypted at rest with a key held by whoever runs this server.
  • If this server, its database, or its encryption key were compromised, your council session could be exposed and misused. Encryption reduces this risk but does not remove it. No online service can promise perfect security.
  • p.stonn does not store your council password. It does store your email, your schedule, and the encrypted session.
  • Your p.stonn login email is verified by a one-time code, and you can only link the council account whose email matches it.
  • Use a password that is unique to your council account, so nothing else of yours is exposed if this service is ever compromised.
  • You can revoke p.stonn's access at any time: disconnect in Settings, or change your council password (which ends the stored session, usually within about half an hour).
  • Consider whether you are comfortable with a third party holding access to your council account before using this. Do not rely on it for anything you can't afford to have go wrong.

Notifications

  • p.stonn attempts to notify you (by email and/or push) each time it changes your permit or fails to. This is best-effort: messages can be delayed, filtered, or lost, and the service can be down. Delivery is not guaranteed.
  • You remain responsible for checking your permit and for any fines. Treat notifications as a convenience, not a guarantee.

No promises

p.stonn is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. It may stop working, make mistakes, or lose data. Use it at your own risk.

Why this exists

Stonnington changed how resident and visitor parking permits work. Since January, permits that used to be free now cost money, and the paper permits anyone could use are being phased out for a digital system. To keep a permit you enter your vehicle registrations into the council's online system, and parking officers check number plates against it.

For visitor parking, that means adding the registration of each car that visits, through the council portal, on the day it visits. For a household that relies on regular help (grandparents on pickup, a nanny, a carer), that becomes a recurring task tied to whoever is coming and when.

p.stonn handles that part for you. You set which car is on your visitor permit on a weekly schedule, add any one-off bookings, and it keeps the council permit up to date and tells you when it has changed.

How it works

A demonstration with made-up plates and names. Not a real permit.

Before you start: get a Stonnington ePermit

p.stonn does not replace the council's system. It works on top of a permit you already hold, so you need a City of Stonnington ePermits account with at least one visitor permit first.

If you are not set up yet:

  • Check you are eligible. Permits are for residents in permit areas, and eligibility depends on your property (generally limited or no off-street parking).
  • Apply through the council. You can apply online, by email, by post, or in person at a customer service centre. You will usually need proof you live at the address and your vehicle details.
  • Set up your online account. The council's digital system is where permits are managed and vehicle registrations are entered.
  • Add a visitor permit. Once your account exists, apply for a visitor permit if you do not already have one. Fees apply, with a discount for concession cardholders.

The council's own pages have the current fees, forms and details:

  • Parking permits
  • Visitor parking permits
  • Digital parking permit FAQs

Once your council account has a visitor permit, sign in to p.stonn and connect it.
